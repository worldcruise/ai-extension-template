Upgrade to Modern highlighting

Enable our new highlighting engine for more stable highlighting on most modern websites, such as LinkedIn, YouTube and Salesforce. It highlights text without changing the page structure, helping prevent duplicated text and leftover highlights as pages update. We recommend enabling Modern highlighting for smoother everyday browsing. Text colors, background colors and underline remain available. Bold, italic, borders, rounded corners and padding aren’t supported in this mode. Your saved styles are kept if you switch back. Enable Modern highlighting in General settings, save, then reload your open pages.
